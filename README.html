Bow Service Marketplace PUBLIC

This is a full private sales storefront that loads inventory from your Google Sheet.

What is included

  • Marketplace-style storefront
  • Google Sheets inventory loading
  • Search, category filters with adult age confirmation, status filters, sorting, and max price filter
  • Featured deals section
  • Product detail modal with image gallery
  • Cart stored locally in the visitor's browser
  • Wishlist stored locally in the visitor's browser
  • Mobile-friendly layout
  • Amazon/eBay external link buttons when your sheet includes links

Image URLs

You can put one image URL or multiple image URLs in the Image URLs column. Separate multiple images with commas, semicolons, or line breaks.

How to change the Google Sheet

  1. Open config.js.
  2. Change sheetId.
  3. Change sheetName if your tab is not named Inventory.
